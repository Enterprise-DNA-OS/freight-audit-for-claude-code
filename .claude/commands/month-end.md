---
description: The month-end freight pack: the accrual for shipped but unbilled freight by cost centre, and billed freight by cost centre.
---

Run `npm run freight -- month-end --json`.

Present the accrual table first with a total per currency, ready to post as a journal (the operator posts it; nothing is written to the accounts system). Then billed freight by cost centre for the last two months. Mention anything unpriced or unbilled for more than 45 days.
