---
description: Prepare for a rate review with one carrier: its error rate, surcharges, lane costs, disputes and rate card expiry, in one brief.
---

For the named carrier, run `scorecard`, `surcharges`, `lane-cost`, `disputes` and `rate-cards` with `--json`, and keep the rows for that carrier.

Write a one-page brief to `drafts/carrier-review-<code>.md`: what they bill wrongly and how often, the surcharges to negotiate, lanes where they cost more per kilo than another carrier, money still owed back, and when the card expires. Numbers only from the commands. Never send it.
